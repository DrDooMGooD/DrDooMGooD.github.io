Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy explains what UGC Ad Factory (the "tool"), run by Domenique Pineda, collects, how it is used, and your choices. The tool runs on my own computer and is used only with TikTok accounts I control.

What the tool collects from TikTok

When a TikTok account is connected, with the account holder's permission, the tool receives:

  • Basic profile information: the account's TikTok ID (open ID), display name, and profile picture, used only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: credentials TikTok issues so the tool can upload videos to that account and check upload status.
  • Upload status: the status TikTok reports for each upload, such as processing, sent to inbox, published, or failed.

The tool does not read the account's followers, messages, other videos, or analytics.

What the tool sends to TikTok

Only videos and captions I have explicitly approved and then confirmed for upload.

Other information the tool handles

To make videos, the tool stores the product briefs, product photos, scripts, and generated media I provide or create. To generate scripts, images, voice, and video, parts of this material are sent to Google (Gemini) and Runway, which process it under their own privacy policies.

How information is stored and protected

  • Everything is stored on my own computer, not on a hosted server.
  • Access tokens are kept out of logs and source code, and are used only to talk to TikTok.
  • Information is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.

How long it is kept

TikTok profile information and tokens are kept while the account is connected and deleted when it is disconnected. Upload records are kept so I can track what was posted, and can be deleted on request.

Your choices

  • Disconnect the account in the tool, which deletes its tokens, or remove the tool's access in the TikTok app under the settings for apps with access to your account.
  • Ask for the information held about your account to be deleted by emailing the address below.

Children

The tool is not intended for anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
