UGC Ad Factory

A private tool run by Domenique Pineda

UGC Ad Factory is a personal tool that helps its owner plan, make, and review short vertical product videos, and then send a finished video to the owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner writes a product brief. The tool drafts ad concepts and scripts, which the owner reviews and edits.
  2. The tool assembles a video from approved scripts. The owner watches it and approves or rejects it.
  3. For an approved video, the owner edits the caption and approves that exact post.
  4. Only then, and only when the owner confirms, the video is uploaded to the owner's TikTok account. Videos are sent to the TikTok inbox, where the owner finishes and posts them in the TikTok app.

Who can use it

The tool runs on its owner's own computer and is not offered to the public. It only connects to TikTok accounts that the owner controls and has signed in with.
